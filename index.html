About Me

Let me introduce myself. I´m a 22 y/o Graphic Design Student based in Alicante (Spain). This is my 3rd year studying and I´m ready to show the world my vision! Hope you enjoy my projects, have fun :)

Editorial

A13 Magazine -> During my second year at university, I worked on A13, a magazine highlighting emerging creatives from Alicante. For this project, my team created all the content from scratch, including research(artists, trends, etc.), the photography used in the interviews and covers, and the overall editorial design.

Merch

Laabelz Tees -> In this personal project, I designed 11 fanart T-shirts as potential merchandise for my current favourite musical artist, under my artistic name, “Laabelz.” I work on this project as a creative break whenever I feel overwhelmed with classwork, and as an experimental space for developing my skills as a graphic designer.

Branding

MGUS (Modular Gardening for Unblown Spaces)-> MGUS comes to life at the crossroads of agronomy and architecture. Cristina and Patri (both current students and researchers at the UPV) needed a visual identity to take their already awarded project to a higher graphic quality level. My role was developing the new image (logo, adaptations...), documents such as submission documents for awards or elevator speeches and merchandising (T-Shirts, presentation cards...).

Photography

Neved Had Sex Concept Session Nº1-> Since I was 13, I enjoyed outdoor photography, but sadly I lost interest in it 6 years ago.The project Concept Session comes from the idea of reconecting with these old hobbies thath I used to enjoy when I was younger. In this first edition, I explore the "Opiumcore" style, focusing on the clothing and the industrial setting
